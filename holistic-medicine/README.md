# Holistic Medicine Skill for Gemini CLI

Comprehensive wellness guidance integrating holistic medicine principles of **Barbara O'Neill** and **Gary Brecka**.

## What This Skill Provides

Expert knowledge in:

* 🌿 **Nutritional Medicine**: Whole foods as primary healing tool
* 🔄 **Detoxification**: Supporting the body's 5 elimination channels
* 💪 **Movement \& Physical Health**: Optimal exercise and circulation
* 😴 **Sleep Optimization**: Restorative rest protocols
* 🧘 **Stress \& Mental Health**: Nervous system regulation
* 🎯 **Biohacking**: Personalized health optimization
* 📊 **Assessment Framework**: Systematic health analysis
* 🌍 **Environmental Factors**: Creating conditions for health

## Key Philosophical Foundations

### Barbara O'Neill Approach

* Body possesses innate healing ability
* Whole foods are medicine
* Natural detoxification processes
* Prevention through lifestyle
* Elimination of root causes, not symptom suppression

### Gary Brecka Approach

* Measure and personalize health optimization
* Data-driven biohacking
* Individual biomarker tracking
* Incremental improvement protocols
* Systems thinking about wellness

## Integration Points

## Health Assessment Framework

When addressing any health concern, this skill guides through:

1. **Nutrition** - What's being consumed?
2. **Digestion** - Is nutrient absorption working?
3. **Elimination** - Are detoxification pathways clear?
4. **Movement** - Is the body activated?
5. **Rest** - Is recovery happening?
6. **Stress** - Is emotional health supported?
7. **Environment** - Are external factors optimal?
