# Elimination Diet Protocol

## Purpose
Identify foods that trigger inflammation, digestive issues, or other symptoms.

## Phase 1: Elimination (3-4 weeks)

**Remove all potential trigger foods:**
- Grains with gluten (wheat, barley, rye)
- Dairy products
- Eggs (optional - leave if well-tolerated)
- Nightshade vegetables (tomato, pepper, eggplant, potato)
- Legumes (may cause digestive difficulty)
- Processed foods, additives, sugar
- Caffeine, alcohol
- Nuts/seeds (optional - reintroduce later)
- Oil except olive/coconut

**What remains:**
- Rice, quinoa, millet, other gluten-free grains
- All vegetables except nightshades
- All fruits (minimize dried for sugar content)
- Olive oil, coconut oil
- Sea salt, herbs, spices
- Herbal teas, water

## Phase 2: Observation (3-4 weeks)

**Document:**
- Energy levels
- Digestion quality
- Respiratory symptoms
- Skin condition
- Mental clarity
- Joint comfort
- Sleep quality
- Mood

**Reintroduce one food family every 5-7 days:**
1. Gluten grains (start with oats, then wheat)
2. Dairy products
3. Nightshades
4. Legumes
5. Nuts/seeds
6. Eggs (if eliminated)
7. Other potential triggers

## Reintroduction Protocol

**For each food:**
1. Add in moderate quantity 2-3 times during 5-day window
2. Observe for 3-5 days for symptoms
3. Document response
4. If no reaction, food is okay
5. If reaction, eliminate and try again in 6 months

**Symptoms to monitor:**
- Digestive: bloating, gas, changes in bowel movement
- Respiratory: congestion, cough
- Skin: itching, eruptions
- Joints: pain or stiffness
- Energy: fatigue, brain fog
- Mood: anxiety, depression
- Sleep: difficulty falling/staying asleep

## Creating Your Personal Template

After elimination phase, your template may look like:

**Core Foods (unlimited):**
- Rice, quinoa, millet
- All vegetables except [your triggers]
- All fruits
- Herbs, spices, sea salt

**Test Foods (occasional/reintroduced successfully):**
- [Foods that passed reintroduction]

**Trigger Foods (eliminate):**
- [Foods causing clear symptoms]

**Variable Foods (individual response):**
- [Foods to test in future]

## Important Notes

- Keep detailed food and symptom journal
- Eliminate completely during phase 1 (even small amounts count)
- Reintroduce one food at a time (can't identify triggers if multiple new foods added)
- Some reactions delayed 48-72 hours
- Food sensitivities may reduce with gut healing; retest in 6-12 months
