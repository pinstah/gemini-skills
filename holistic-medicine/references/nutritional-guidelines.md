# Nutritional Guidelines by Health Condition

## Energy & Vitality
**Eliminate**: Refined sugars, processed foods, excess stimulants
**Emphasize**: 
- Complex carbohydrates (whole grains, legumes)
- High-quality plant proteins
- Iron-rich foods (dark leafy greens, legumes)
- B-complex foods (whole grains, nutritional yeast)
- Omega-3 sources (flaxseeds, chia seeds, walnuts)

**Sample Daily**: Raw salad with greens, legumes, seeds → Whole grain with vegetables → Fresh fruit/smoothie → Herbal tea

## Immune Support
**Eliminate**: Sugar (suppresses immunity), processed foods, excess alcohol
**Emphasize**:
- Citrus fruits (vitamin C)
- Leafy greens (nutrients for white blood cells)
- Garlic, onions (antimicrobial compounds)
- Turmeric, ginger (anti-inflammatory)
- Mushrooms (immune-enhancing beta-glucans)
- Whole grains (maintain gut barrier)

**Practical**: Rainbow salad daily, herbal teas, fresh juices, minimally processed

## Inflammation & Joint Health
**Remove**: Processed foods, excess omega-6 oils, sugar
**Add**:
- Turmeric, ginger, cayenne
- Leafy greens (magnesium, phytonutrients)
- Berries, cherries (anthocyanins)
- Fatty fish alternative (algae omega-3, flaxseeds)
- Whole grains (resistant starch)

**Movement**: Gentle yoga, swimming, walking essential

## Digestive Health
**Support**: Stomach acid with lemon water before meals
**Foods**: Leafy greens, slow carbs, fermented foods
**Chew thoroughly**: All meals, minimum 20-30 chews
**Avoid**: Food combining mistakes, eating stressed, late heavy meals
**Timing**: Fruit alone, vegetables with whole grains/legumes

## Detoxification Support
**Focus**: Support the 5 elimination channels
**Emphasize**: 
- High-water content foods (hydration)
- High-fiber foods (colon support)
- Leafy greens (liver support, chlorophyll)
- Garlic, onions (sulfur compounds)
- Brassicas (broccoli, cabbage - sulforaphane)
- Citrus (limonene, vitamin C)

**Hydration**: 2-3L filtered water daily
**Fasting**: 12-16 hour overnight fasts support autophagy

## Weight Management
**Not calorie-counting, but nutrient density**
**Emphasize**: 
- Whole foods that satisfy nutritionally
- Adequate fiber (satiety, metabolic health)
- Adequate plant proteins
- Blood sugar stabilization (whole grains, legumes, seeds)

**Critical**: Address emotional eating, stress, sleep
**Movement**: Strength training preserves muscle during transition

## Skin Health
**External reflects internal**
**Emphasize**: 
- High-water foods (hydration)
- Antioxidant foods (berries, leafy greens)
- Healthy fats (seeds, nuts)
- Sulfur foods (garlic, onions, brassicas)
- Hydration and sweating (through exercise)

**Eliminate**: Sugar, processed oils, excess caffeine, stress

---

**Remember**: Individual food sensitivities vary. Use elimination diet to identify personal triggers.
